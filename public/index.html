How to get it

Three easy ways to enjoy your cheesecake

Every order is made fresh for the week. Order by Saturday at 6 PM and it's yours to enjoy the following week.

Tuesday or Wednesday

Meet us on the route

Free. We deliver to shops from Waterville to South Portland every week. Pick a stop on our route and meet us there on delivery day. We'll contact you to confirm the date and time. You must meet the driver in person: we can't leave custom orders at the store.

Wednesday to Saturday

Pick up at the BUB Shed

1160 Lewiston Road in Litchfield, 9 AM to 5 PM. The Shed is for pickup of pre-ordered cheesecakes only. We'll contact you to confirm the date and time.

Ships Wednesday

Ship minis anywhere in Maine

Mini packs ship out on Wednesday by UPS and arrive in 24 to 48 hours within the State of Maine. Flat $19.95 shipping. Whole cheesecakes are pickup or route only.

Our story

A small family business with a big flavor board

Batter Up Bakery is a family bakery in Litchfield that specializes in one thing: decadent cheesecake. We bake dozens of flavors, add new inspired and seasonal ones to keep it fun, and drive them out to shops all over Maine every week.

We're grateful to every market, pub and country store that shares our cheesecakes with you, and to everyone who supports our small family business. Thank you!
